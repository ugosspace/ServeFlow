Core Features

Worker Scheduling

Assign workers to specific service days and ministry roles. Weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly. Always know who's on duty.

  • Weekly & monthly schedules
  • Department-specific assignments
  • Easy reassignment
🔄

Rotation Timetable Generator

Generate a full month's service rotation in seconds. Fair, automatic, and shareable instantly to WhatsApp.

  • Auto-generate fair rotations
  • One-click PDF/image export
  • Multi-service support

Attendance Tracking

Mark workers present, absent, late, or excused — right from your phone on service day. Build accountability over time.

  • Present / Absent / Late / Excused

Workers receive automatic WhatsApp/SMS reminders before their scheduled service. No more "I forgot" excuses.

  • WhatsApp + SMS delivery

  • Worker absence submission

  • Auto-notify replacement
